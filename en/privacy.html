2. In short

  • The App is made for young children but collects no data from the child: no accounts, names, photos, microphone, location, ads or behavioural analytics.
  • Drawings are stored only on the device. We do not upload them to any server and cannot access them.
  • The internet is needed only to handle Premium purchases (through the Apple App Store and the provider RevenueCat). Purchases are made by a parent, behind a parental gate.

3. What we process and why

3.1. Data that stays on the device (never sent to us)

Data Purpose Where
The child's drawings (colours, strokes) and colouring progress Saving work and returning to it Files in the App's private directory on the device
Sound settings Remembering choices App local storage (UserDefaults)

This data is removed when the App is deleted. We never receive it. The App does not copy drawings to iCloud; however a device backup made by Apple (if the parent enabled it) may include the App's data — that is a parent's setting in iOS.

3.2. Data processed for Premium purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to handle purchases. Payments are processed by Apple — we receive no card number, payment details, or the parent's name and address.

When a parent opens the offer, makes or restores a purchase, or the App checks entitlements, RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous, random app user identifier (not linked to a name, email or Apple account);
  • transaction data and the Apple receipt (product and transaction identifiers, dates, subscription status);
  • basic technical data: device type and OS version, App version, store language/region, last-seen time;
  • the IP address, which is inevitably visible in any network connection.

Purposes: handling purchases, verifying and granting Premium, restoring purchases, abuse prevention and basic sales statistics (revenue, subscriber counts) — with no profiling of children and no advertising.

In the App we do not set name, email or other user attributes, and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

The paywall and subscription management screens may be loaded from RevenueCat's infrastructure and shown inside the App; RevenueCat may record technical events related to showing the offer.

3.3. Contacting us

If you write to kszubski@gmail.com, we process your email address and message to reply.

3.4. What we do not do

We use no ads, no analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase, Google Analytics), no cross-app tracking, no user accounts and no server of our own. We do not "track" users in the sense of Apple's App Tracking Transparency, so no ATT prompt is shown.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract: handling the purchase and granting Premium to the parent who uses it; restoring a purchase.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interests: preventing abuse and fraud, security and operation of the service, defending claims, answering messages.
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — legal obligation: accounting and tax duties (Apple acts as the merchant settling the transaction; we receive sales reports without data identifying the buyer).

We do not rely on consent, so there is no consent to withdraw.

  • Apple Inc. / Apple Distribution International — seller and operator of payments and App distribution, acting as a separate controller under its own terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
  • Email providers and the host of the legal pages (GitHub Pages) — to the extent you visit that site or email us.
  • Public authorities — where required by law.

We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Data sent to RevenueCat is processed in the USA. The transfer relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, included in RevenueCat's data processing terms (DPA). You can obtain a copy of the safeguards by writing to kszubski@gmail.com.

7. Retention

  • On the device: until the App or drawings are deleted.
  • RevenueCat: transaction data and the anonymous identifier are kept while we use the service, then according to RevenueCat's policy — per its privacy policy, up to 6 years to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. On our request, a given user's data can be deleted.
  • Correspondence: up to 12 months after the matter is closed, longer if needed to defend claims.
  • Accounting records: as required by law (generally 5 years from the end of the tax year).

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, https://uodo.gov.pl) or the authority in your country.

Because we look up data in RevenueCat by an anonymous identifier, we may ask for additional information (e.g. a transaction number from the Apple receipt). We do not store drawings — delete them by deleting the App or choosing "Start over" on a picture.

Providing data when purchasing is voluntary, but without it Premium cannot be bought or restored. We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Children

The App is aimed at children aged 3–6, but a parent or guardian makes purchases; purchase functions are protected by a parental gate and require confirmation by Apple. The App:

  • collects no personal data from children and never asks for name, age, email, photo, voice or location (saving the child's own picture to the device's Photos happens locally and is not data collection);
  • contains no ads, no outbound links and no purchases reachable without the parental gate;
  • has no social or chat features.

Therefore we do not collect "personal information from children" within the meaning of the US COPPA and do not rely on parental consent under Art. 8 GDPR (in Poland the age threshold is 16). If you believe a child's data has nevertheless reached us, write to kszubski@gmail.com — we will promptly check and delete it.

10. Security and changes

Communication with RevenueCat uses encrypted HTTPS. Drawings live in the App's private directory protected by iOS.

We will announce material changes in the App (parent area) and on the policy page; the current version is always at https://kszubski.github.io/tosia/en/privacy.html.
